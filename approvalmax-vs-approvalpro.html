ApprovalMax vs ApprovalPro: Xero Approval Software Compared

Both tools add multi-level approval to Xero and QuickBooks. Here’s an honest look at pricing, features, and who each one suits, so you can pick the right fit for your team.

Last reviewed 26 September 2026

The short answer

Choose ApprovalMax if…

  • You run Oracle NetSuite, or need approvals across many document types
  • You want native iOS and Android approval apps
  • You want to pay suppliers from the same tool, via its Pay add-on
  • You prefer an established vendor used by 20,000+ businesses

Choose ApprovalPro if…

  • You want to start free, with multi-tier approval included
  • You want one flat price, with budget checks and AI invoice capture included
  • You want spend approved at the purchase request, before a PO exists
  • You want support from the team who builds it, in your time zone

Where ApprovalMax stands out

ApprovalMax is a mature product with a wide footprint. It supports Oracle NetSuite as well as Xero and QuickBooks Online, covers more document types such as credit notes, expenses, and supplier onboarding, includes unlimited users on every plan, and offers native mobile apps for approving on the go.

Its Pay add-on lets you pay suppliers from the same place you approve them, and its Premium plan includes phone support and API access. If those are must-haves, ApprovalMax is a strong choice.
