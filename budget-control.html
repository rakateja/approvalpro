What is budget control?

Budget control means checking spend against a set budget before it’s approved.

Most teams only find out they’ve gone over budget at month end, when the money is already spent. ApprovalPro puts a budget check on the document itself, so the person submitting and the person approving can both see whether there’s room in the budget.

Where the budget check appears

A budget checking section shows up on three types of documents.

Expense claim submission

When staff submit an expense claim, they see the budget check before sending it for approval.

Vendor bill

Each vendor bill is checked against budget, so approvers know the impact before it goes to Xero or QuickBooks.

Purchase request

Purchase requests are checked before any money is committed, while there’s still time to say no.

How it works

  1. Set your budget

    Set the budget for each account or department.

  2. Submit

    Staff submit an expense claim, vendor bill, or purchase request.

  3. Check

    The budget check shows whether the spend fits within budget.

  4. Approve

    Approvers decide with the budget in front of them. Approved documents sync to your books.
